Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

When you interact with Nexic Web, we collect data to provide you with the ultimate premium digital experience. The information we gather falls into two core categories:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, billing address, and payment information required for seamless checkout.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and navigational data across our templates.

How We Use Data

Your data powers our digital infrastructure. We utilize collected information strictly to enhance your experience and deliver value:

  • To deliver, maintain, and secure the premium templates you purchase.
  • To process transactions securely via our enterprise payment partners.
  • To send critical updates, security alerts, and administrative notifications.
  • To analyze usage metrics and continuously refine our UI/UX standards.

Cookies Policy

Nexic Web utilizes advanced tracking technologies to ensure a frictionless browsing experience. These text files allow us to remember your preferences and cart contents across sessions.

You reserve the right to configure your browser to reject non-essential cookies; however, doing so may limit the functionality of certain premium features on our platform.

Third-Party Services

To operate at a global standard, we integrate with industry-leading third-party services. These partners are bound by strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Payment Gateways: Processing handled securely via Stripe & PayPal.
  • Analytics: Usage diagnostics provided by Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics.
  • Hosting: Cloud infrastructure maintained by Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Data Protection

Security is embedded in our DNA. We protect your data using military-grade AES-256 encryption both in transit and at rest. Our infrastructure undergoes regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments to thwart unauthorized access, alteration, or data destruction.

Data Sharing Policy

We do not monetize your privacy. Nexic Web never sells, rents, or trades your personal information to third parties. Data is only shared when legally compelled by a court of law or when explicitly necessary to fulfill a service you requested (e.g., passing address details to a payment processor).

User Rights

You retain full sovereignty over your personal data. Under global privacy frameworks (GDPR, CCPA), you hold the right to:

  • Request a comprehensive export of all data we hold regarding your account.
  • Rectify any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Request total erasure of your digital footprint from our servers ("Right to be Forgotten").

Policy Updates

The digital landscape evolves, and so do our policies. We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at our discretion. Significant changes will be communicated via email to registered users and prominently highlighted on this page via the "Last Updated" timestamp.
