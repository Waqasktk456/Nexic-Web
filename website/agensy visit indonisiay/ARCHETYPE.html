Operational Guidelines FAQ

Important procedural notes regarding initial site reviews, creative autonomy limits, and engineering structural collaboration pipelines.

We prioritize spatial integrity over raw surface scale, however, our master systems are optimized for structures starting at 250 square meters to guarantee appropriate room for geometric spatial flow.

Studio Archetype supplies standardized vector layouts and exhaustive material technical specs. Local certified structural engineering partners verify regional weight load metrics and seismic code compliance parameters.
