13 / About the atelier
Space. Material. Image. Identity. Direction.

Atelier Noir is a multidisciplinary practice for the moments when a project needs more than a discipline. We move between architecture, interiors, fashion and creative direction to find the idea that can hold them together.

Small by design, international by nature. We work with founders, cultural institutions and houses who understand that the most valuable outcome is not attention. It is memory.
