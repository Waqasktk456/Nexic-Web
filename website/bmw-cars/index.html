THE LEGACY

1972

BMW Motorsport GmbH is established. The legendary 3.0 CSL dominates the touring car scene.

1978

The M1 is born. The first official M car and a mid-engine supercar masterpiece.

1986

The first BMW M3 (E30) revolutionizes the sports car market, becoming the most successful touring car ever.

2024

Electrification meets M Power. The XM and i4 M50 pave the way for a high-voltage future.
