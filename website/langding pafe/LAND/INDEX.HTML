FAQ

Questions we hear often

How long does a typical project take?
Most projects take 8 to 14 weeks from kickoff to launch. Simple landing pages can ship in 4 to 6 weeks, while full-scale platforms with custom features typically take 12 to 20 weeks. We'll give you a firm timeline during our discovery phase.
What does the design process look like?
We start with a discovery workshop to understand your business, audience, and goals. From there, we move into research, wireframes, and high-fidelity mockups. You'll see interactive prototypes before we write any code, so there are no surprises at launch.
Do you work with existing brands or only new ones?
Both. About 60% of our projects involve redesigning existing websites or evolving established brands. We're equally comfortable building from scratch or working within existing brand guidelines.
What's included in the pricing?
Every plan includes strategy, design, development, testing, deployment, and post-launch support. We don't charge extra for revisions during the design phase (up to 3 rounds) or for basic CMS training. Hosting and domain registration are separate.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes. We typically structure payments as 40% upfront, 30% at design approval, and 30% at launch. For larger projects, we can arrange custom payment schedules. We accept wire transfer, credit card, and crypto.
What if I need something not listed in the plans?
The plans are starting points. Most clients need some customization. Tell us what you're building and we'll put together a proposal that fits your exact requirements and budget.
What happens after launch?
Every project includes post-launch support (30 to 90 days depending on your plan). During this period, we fix bugs, make minor adjustments, and monitor performance. After that, you can continue with a retainer or handle things independently with the documentation and training we provide.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Yes. We offer monthly retainer packages for ongoing development, content updates, performance monitoring, and feature additions. About 70% of our clients move to a retainer after their initial project.
Will I be able to update content myself?
Absolutely. We integrate a content management system (CMS) into every project so you can update text, images, and basic layouts without touching code. We also provide training and documentation.
