How We Work

Four weeks from kickoff to first deliverable

Our process is designed for velocity without sacrificing depth. Every engagement follows this arc, adapted to the problem at hand.

Immersion

Deep-dive into your business, market, users, and existing systems. We surface constraints and opportunities others miss.

2

Architecture

Define the strategic framework, information architecture, and technical approach. Decisions made here compound throughout the project.
