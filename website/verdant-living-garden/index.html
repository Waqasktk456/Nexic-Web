OUR STORY / 014

WE START WITH OBSERVATION.

VERDANT began with a belief that the best gardens do not announce themselves. They settle into a life, creating small daily rituals that become impossible to imagine without.

Our work sits between planting design, landscape architecture, and the simple act of paying attention. We build places with enough structure to last and enough freedom to keep changing.
